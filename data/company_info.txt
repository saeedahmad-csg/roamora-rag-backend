Booking a trip with Roamora Travels is fast and straightforward:
1. Choose Your Package or Destination: Browse our catalog (Hunza, Skardu, Swat, Dubai, Bali, Istanbul, Paris) or request a custom itinerary.
2. Inquire or Click "Plan Your Trip": Click the "Plan Your Trip" button on the website, email hello@roamoratravels.com, or message our consultant in this chat with your travel dates and party size.

## 6. Cancellation, Rescheduling & Refund Policy
We understand that travel plans can change unexpectedly. Our cancellation policy is as follows:
- 30+ Days Before Departure: Full refund of total trip cost minus a flat $50 administrative fee and non-recoverable hotel deposits.
- 15 to 29 Days Before Departure: 50% refund of the total package price.
- 14 Days or Less Before Departure: Non-refundable due to pre-paid hotel commitments, driver retainers, and attraction passes.
- Free Date Rescheduling: You may reschedule your trip dates once free of charge up to 14 days prior to departure, subject to seasonal hotel availability.
- Force Majeure & Weather Delays: If a tour is impacted by natural disasters, roadblocks (e.g. Karakoram Highway landslides), or flight cancellations, Roamora Travels provides alternate scenic routes or credits for future travel.

## 7. Luggage, Transport & Packing Rules
- Baggage Allowance: For road trips and mountain expeditions, each traveler is allowed 1 large suitcase/duffel bag (up to 20kg) plus 1 small personal daypack.
- Transport Vehicles: We use late-model air-conditioned Toyota Prado / Land Cruisers for mountain routes, luxury private vans for city transfers, and private chauffeurs.
- What to Pack for Mountain Tours (Hunza, Skardu, Swat): Warm fleece/jacket for evenings, comfortable hiking shoes, sunglasses, sunblock, and universal power adapters.

---

## 8. Travel Insurance & Health Guidelines
- Travel Insurance: Comprehensive international travel and medical evacuation insurance is strongly recommended for all international travelers and mandatory for mountain trekking in northern Pakistan.
- High-Altitude Destinations (Hunza & Skardu): Altitudes range from 2,200m to 2,500m. Travelers are advised to stay hydrated and take an acclimatization rest day. We provide oxygen cylinders in our transport vehicles during mountain expeditions.
- Dietary Requirements: Halal, vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free dietary requests can be accommodated with 7 days advance notice.

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
- Q: How do I book a tour or package?
  A: Simply click "Plan Your Trip" on our website, email hello@roamoratravels.com, or let us know in this chat! A 30% deposit secures your booking.

  A: We create custom itineraries for any destination! Let our team know your preferred dates, budget, and group size, and we will craft a personalized itinerary within 24 hours.
- Q: Can I customize an existing package?
  A: Absolutely! All packages can be extended, shortened, upgraded to 5-star luxury hotels, or adjusted for private solo/family groups.
- Q: Are meals and hotels included in packages?
  A: Yes! All standard packages include daily breakfasts, selective traditional dinners (or full board on northern circuit expeditions), and vetted 4-star/5-star or heritage accommodations.
- Q: Are international flights included in the package price?
  A: Standard package prices cover all ground accommodations, transfers, activities, and domestic flights where specified. International flights can be booked upon request through our ticketing desk.
- Q: What languages do tour guides speak?
  A: All our tour leaders and guides are fluent in English and local languages (Urdu, Arabic, Turkish, French, Indonesian depending on region). Multilingual guides (German, Spanish, Chinese) are available upon advance request.
- Q: How do I get in touch with my guide?
  A: 48 hours prior to arrival, you will receive a WhatsApp message connecting you directly with your lead tour guide and driver.
- Q: What is your cancellation and refund policy?
  A: Cancellations made 30+ days before departure receive a full refund minus a $50 admin fee. Cancellations 15-29 days before departure receive a 50% refund. Cancellations within 14 days are non-refundable. You can reschedule once for free up to 14 days before departure.
