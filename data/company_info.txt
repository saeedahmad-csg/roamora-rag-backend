# Lumina Direct E-Commerce Store - Official Customer & Store Policies

## 1. About Lumina Direct
Lumina Direct (www.luminamart.com) is a premium direct-to-consumer e-commerce retailer offering cutting-edge electronics, smart home gadgets, lifestyle tech, and high-performance accessories. Founded in 2021, Lumina Direct operates fulfillment hubs in Indianapolis, IN and Reno, NV, delivering across North America and over 40 countries worldwide.

- Brand Slogan: "Smarter Technology, Brighter Living."
- Customer Happiness Guarantee: Every order is backed by a 30-day money-back guarantee and dedicated 24/7 customer care.

## 2. Shipping & Delivery Guidelines
We offer fast, insured shipping on all orders with multiple delivery tiers:

- Free Standard Shipping: Automatically applied to all orders with a subtotal of $50.00 or more (after discounts, before taxes). Takes 3 to 5 business days. For orders under $50, standard shipping is a flat $4.99.
- Expedited 2-Day Shipping: $9.99 flat rate. Guarantees delivery within 2 business days if placed before 2:00 PM EST.
- Priority Overnight Delivery: $19.99 flat rate. Guarantees next-business-day delivery for orders placed before 2:00 PM EST Monday through Friday.
- Order Dispatch Cutoff: Orders placed before 2:00 PM Eastern Time (Monday-Friday) are processed and shipped the same business day. Orders placed over the weekend or on federal holidays ship the next business day.
- Carrier Partners: We ship domestically via FedEx, UPS, and USPS Priority Mail.
- International Shipping: Available to over 40 countries via DHL Express (estimated 6-10 business days). Duties and import taxes are calculated and collected upfront at checkout (DDP - Delivered Duty Paid), so there are zero surprise fees upon arrival.

## 3. Order Tracking, Modifications & Cancellations
- Order Confirmation: Immediately after placing an order, an email with your 8-digit Order Number (e.g., #LUM-84920) and order summary is dispatched.
- Tracking Information: A tracking link is automatically emailed once the carrier scans your package. You can also track packages anytime on our website under "Track My Order".
- Order Changes & Cancellations: Because orders are transmitted rapidly to our automated fulfillment center, cancellations or shipping address adjustments must be submitted within 60 minutes of placing the order via our Live Chat or Emergency Helpline.

## 4. 30-Day Returns, Exchanges & Refund Policy
We want you to love your purchase. If you are not completely satisfied, you may initiate a return within 30 days of package delivery:

- Return Eligibility Window: 30 calendar days from the date of confirmed carrier delivery.
- Item Condition: Returned items must be in brand new, original condition, including the manufacturer box, manuals, cables, power adapters, and warranty registration cards.
- Free Exchanges & Store Credit: Return shipping is 100% FREE if you choose an item exchange or Lumina Store Credit.
- Refunds to Original Payment Method: For refunds back to your credit card, PayPal, or bank account, a flat $5.99 return shipping and restocking fee is deducted from the refund total.
- Defective or Damaged Upon Arrival: If an item arrives damaged in transit or is dead on arrival (DOA), notify customer support within 48 hours of delivery. We will issue a prepaid overnight return label and dispatch an immediate brand-new replacement at zero cost to you.
- Refund Processing Timeline: Once our warehouse receives and inspects the return (typically 2-3 business days), refunds are processed to your original payment method. Depending on your financial institution, funds appear on your bank statement within 3 to 5 business days.
- Non-Returnable Items: Digital gift cards, downloadable software licenses, customized/engraved devices, and hygiene-sensitive opened earphones/earbuds (unless verified defective).

## 5. Payment Options & Checkout Security
We offer safe, tokenized, and encrypted checkout options:
- Credit / Debit Cards: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, JCB.
- Digital Wallets: Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay, PayPal One-Touch.
- Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL): 
  - Klarna: Split your purchase into 4 interest-free payments due every 2 weeks.
  - Afterpay: Available for orders between $35 and $1,500.
- Payment Security: 256-bit SSL encryption, PCI-DSS Level 1 certified checkout. We never store raw credit card numbers on our servers.

## 6. Lumina Rewards & VIP Loyalty Program
Our free rewards program lets you earn perks on every purchase:
- Point Accumulation: Earn 1 Lumina Point for every $1 spent.
- Redemption: Every 100 Points can be redeemed for a $5.00 discount voucher at checkout.
- Welcome Bonus: Receive 50 bonus points upon creating a free account.
- Birthday Special: Receive an exclusive 15% off discount code during your birthday month.
- Lumina VIP Gold Tier: Automatically unlocked when you spend $500 or more in a calendar year. Benefits include free 2-day shipping on all orders without minimum spend, priority customer support routing, and 24-hour early access to Black Friday and seasonal flash sales.

## 7. Product Warranty & LuminaCare Protection
- Standard Manufacturer Warranty: All brand-new hardware products include a 1-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty covering electrical failure, internal component breakdown, and factory defects.
- LuminaCare Extended Protection (Optional at Checkout):
  - 2-Year Plan ($29.99): Extends coverage to 2 full years and includes accidental damage protection (drops, cracked screens, liquid spills).
  - 3-Year Plan ($49.99): Extends coverage to 3 full years, includes battery replacement if health drops below 80%, zero deductible, and express replacement courier service.
